# College coach email subject lines (examples for every sport)

Before a coach reads your email, they see the subject line. It should tell them who you are, what you play and when you graduate, so they can sort and search it later. This page has a simple formula, real-looking examples for 12 sports, and subject lines for follow-ups and updates. Every name, school and number below is made up.

Need the email itself? Use our [templates for your sport](https://judestrahm.github.io/college-coach-email-templates/) or our free [College Coach Email Generator](https://sentry-sell-recruit.judestrahm.workers.dev/).

## The formula

```text
[Grad year] [Position or event] | [Height/weight or best mark] | [High school] ([City, ST]) | [Film/Results/Times] inside
```

Why each part is there:

1. **Grad year first.** Coaches recruit by class, so "2028" tells them right away which year you belong to.
2. **Position or event.** Use the short form coaches use in your sport (RHP, OLB, 400m, 200 Fly).
3. **One number that matters.** Height and weight for size positions, a time or mark for results sports.
4. **School, city and state.** It shows where you are and helps them plan to see you play.
5. **What is inside.** "Film inside" or "Times inside" says there is something to click.

Phones cut long subject lines off, so put grad year and position first, where they always show. No all caps, no emojis, no "PLEASE READ" or "Future star".

## Follow-ups and updates

Coaches are busy, and no answer to a first email is common. A short follow-up with something new is normal. Keep your grad year and position in every subject line so the whole conversation stays easy to find.

```text
Follow-up:          Following up: 2028 WR, Central HS (Wichita, KS)
New film:           New film: 2028 WR | Central HS (Wichita, KS) | Week 6 vs North
New PR or time:     New PR: 2028 Sprints | 200m 21.8 | Central HS (Wichita, KS)
Schedule:           Fall schedule: 2028 CM | Central HS (Wichita, KS)
Before a camp:      Attending your [date] camp: 2028 OH | Central HS (Wichita, KS)
After a camp:       Thank you for camp: 2028 RHP | Central HS (Wichita, KS)
Tournament or meet: Playing at [event] [dates]: 2028 C | Central HS (Wichita, KS)
```

## Replying to a coach

When a coach writes back, hit reply and keep their subject line. Changing it can split the thread in the coach's inbox. For what to write in the reply, see [How to reply to a college coach's email](replying-to-college-coaches.md).

## Subject lines to avoid

- **"Recruiting" or "Prospect" alone.** Says nothing a coach can sort by.
- **"Hi Coach" or a blank subject.** Easy to skip, impossible to find later.
- **Your name only.** The coach does not know your name yet. Lead with grad year and position.
- **Hype words** like "D1 talent", "must see" or "elite". Let the numbers and film speak.
- **The same subject to every school with the wrong coach's name in it.** Check before you send.

## Quick checklist before you hit send

1. Grad year and position are in the subject line.
2. The film, results or times link works on a phone, without a login.
3. The coach's name and email match the school's athletics staff page.
4. You are sending from an email address you check every day.
5. You wrote down the school, coach and date so you know when to follow up.
