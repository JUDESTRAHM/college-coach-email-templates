## What every good first email has

1. A subject line a coach can sort by: grad year, position, size or key mark, school, city and state.
2. Your film, results or times link near the top.
3. Your one to three best numbers and your GPA.
4. One true, specific reason you want that program.
5. A coach or reference who can vouch for you, and how to reach you.

Keep it short enough to read on a phone. Send it yourself from your own email.
