# Email template for a college swimming coach

Free, copy-paste recruiting email for high school swimming athletes (and parents helping them). Three parts: a filled-in sample so you can see the shape, a blank template, and a follow-up for when nobody writes back.

Want it filled in for you? The free generator for swimming does it in your browser, nothing is stored: **[https://sentry-sell-recruit.judestrahm.workers.dev/swimming/](https://sentry-sell-recruit.judestrahm.workers.dev/swimming/)**

## 1. Sample (made-up player)

This is a fictional player, not a real recruit. Notice the order: who you are, your times link near the top, your numbers, one real reason you like the program, then a reference.

```text
Subject: 2028 Fly | 5'8" | Central HS (Wichita, KS) | Times inside

Coach Johnson,

I'm Lily Chen, a 2028 butterflyer at Central High School in Wichita, KS. I'm 5'8".

Times: https://your-times-link

SCY bests: 100 fly 55.80, 200 fly 2:03.90. GPA 3.9.

I watched your 200 fly swimmers at last season's conference meet, and your butterfly group is the kind I want to train with.

I'd appreciate it if you'd take a look at my times. My coach, Coach Ruiz (coach.ruiz@example.com), can speak to my work ethic and character.

Thank you for your time,
Lily Chen
Fly | Class of 2028 | Central HS
lily@example.com
```

## 2. Blank template

```text
Subject: [Grad year] [Position or event] | [Height/weight] | [High school] ([City, State]) | Times inside

Coach [Last name],

I'm [First Last], a [grad year] [position or event] at [High school] in [City, State]. I'm [height/weight].

Times: [link]

[Your 1-3 best numbers for swimming]. GPA [x.x].

[One specific, true reason you want THIS program. Name something you saw.]

My coach, Coach [Name] ([email or phone]), can speak to my work ethic and character.

Thank you for your time,
[First Last]
[Position] | Class of [year] | [High school]
[your email] | [your phone]
```

## 3. Follow-up (about two weeks later, only if no reply)

```text
Subject: Following up: [Grad year] [Position or event], [High school]

Coach [Last name],

I wanted to follow up on my email from a couple of weeks ago. I'm a [grad year] [position or event] at [High school] and I'm very interested in [College].

Times: [link]

Quick numbers: [your best 1-2 numbers, updated if you have new ones].

If there's anything else you need from me, or a meet you'd like to see me swim at, I'd be glad to hear it.

Thank you,
[First Last]
```

## Quick rules of thumb

- Email the coach who recruits your event group (sprint free, distance, fly and so on), plus the recruiting coordinator. Find them on the school's athletics staff directory. Don't send the whole staff the same message.
- Put your times link in the first few lines. Coaches read on their phones.
- Make the "why this program" line true and specific. A coach can tell when it was pasted into 50 emails.
- Send from an email address you check, and keep a list of who you emailed and when.
