# Email template for a college tennis coach

Free, copy-paste recruiting email for high school tennis athletes (and parents helping them). Three parts: a filled-in sample so you can see the shape, a blank template, and a follow-up for when nobody writes back.

Want it filled in for you? The free generator for tennis does it in your browser, nothing is stored: **[https://sentry-sell-recruit.judestrahm.workers.dev/tennis/](https://sentry-sell-recruit.judestrahm.workers.dev/tennis/)**

## 1. Sample (made-up player)

This is a fictional player, not a real recruit. Notice the order: who you are, your video link near the top, your numbers, one real reason you like the program, then a reference.

```text
Subject: 2028 S/D | 5'11" | Central HS (Wichita, KS) | Video inside

Coach Johnson,

I'm Noah Patel, a 2028 singles and doubles player at Central High School in Wichita, KS. I'm 5'11".

Video: https://your-video-link

UTR 10.2; #1 singles for my high school as a junior. GPA 3.7.

I like how your doubles teams attack the net. That's how I like to play.

I'd appreciate it if you'd take a look at my video. My coach, Coach Ruiz (coach.ruiz@example.com), can speak to my work ethic and character.

Thank you for your time,
Noah Patel
S/D | Class of 2028 | Central HS
noah@example.com
```

## 2. Blank template

```text
Subject: [Grad year] [Position or event] | [Height/weight] | [High school] ([City, State]) | Video inside

Coach [Last name],

I'm [First Last], a [grad year] [position or event] at [High school] in [City, State]. I'm [height/weight].

Video: [link]

[Your 1-3 best numbers for tennis]. GPA [x.x].

[One specific, true reason you want THIS program. Name something you saw.]

My coach, Coach [Name] ([email or phone]), can speak to my work ethic and character.

Thank you for your time,
[First Last]
[Position] | Class of [year] | [High school]
[your email] | [your phone]
```

## 3. Follow-up (about two weeks later, only if no reply)

```text
Subject: Following up: [Grad year] [Position or event], [High school]

Coach [Last name],

I wanted to follow up on my email from a couple of weeks ago. I'm a [grad year] [position or event] at [High school] and I'm very interested in [College].

Video: [link]

Quick numbers: [your best 1-2 numbers, updated if you have new ones].

If there's anything else you need from me, or a camp you'd like to see me at, I'd be glad to hear it.

Thank you,
[First Last]
```

## Quick rules of thumb

- Email the assistant coach who recruits your position or area, plus the recruiting coordinator. Find them on the school's athletics staff directory. Don't send the whole staff the same message.
- Put your video link in the first few lines. Coaches read on their phones.
- Make the "why this program" line true and specific. A coach can tell when it was pasted into 50 emails.
- Send from an email address you check, and keep a list of who you emailed and when.
